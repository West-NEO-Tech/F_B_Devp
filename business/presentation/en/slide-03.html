Executive Summary: What is BizSim?
◎ Core Positioning
BizSim is a startup validation platform powered by large-scale multi-agent market simulation. Entrepreneurs describe their product, and the system auto-generates thousands of diverse AI agents that freely interact, compete, and produce emergent market dynamics — delivering a complete market simulation in minutes.
🔑 Core Value
Before investing real money, "launch" your product in a digital sandbox — what you see isn't a single AI's opinion, but authentic feedback from a virtual market. Generate multi-dimensional business viability reports including adoption curves, sentiment analysis, conversion funnels, and unit economics.
